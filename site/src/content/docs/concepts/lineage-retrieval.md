---
title: Lineage and retrieval
description: Follow symbols and structural similarity across repository history.
---

Historian records declarations, references, diagnostics, and normalized structural features. Exact identity, fuzzy lineage, and structural similarity can then connect symbols across renames and edits without requiring an external model.

Use `history` for a symbol timeline, `changes` for relevant revisions, `similar` for structural neighbors, `retrieve` for broader historical context, and `trace` for bounded lineage paths.
